Questions & Answers

What is Forever Growth?

Forever Growth is a hair care company that sells shampoo, conditioner, and hair oil to help support healthy hair growth.

What hair types can use these products?

Our products are made for all hair types, including sensitive and non-sensitive hair.

How often should I use the products?

You can use the shampoo and conditioner on wash days, and the hair oil a few times a week to help keep your scalp moisturized.

Where is Forever Growth based?

Forever Growth is based in Richmond,VA.
